### Pre-Study: Blockchain From Scratch

Neither of us had much background in blockchain, so we sat down and studied before the conference.

The core topics we went through:

- **Solidity** — the language used to write smart contracts on Ethereum
- **Perp DEX** — decentralized perpetual futures exchanges, where you can trade leveraged positions on crypto prices without a centralized exchange
- **RWA (Real World Assets)** — tokenizing physical assets like real estate or bonds on a blockchain
- **Stablecoins** — crypto assets designed to hold a stable value, commonly used for payments and settlement

The rough mental model we built: AI is the brain — it judges, automates, and acts. Web3 is the rail — it handles value, ownership, and settlement. Together, they form a stack where AI agents can operate in financial environments without needing traditional intermediaries.

![image](/images/posts/ai-infracon-blockchain-security/solidity-prep.png)

Pre-studying Solidity before the conference.

It was a lot to absorb in a short time, but having this foundation made the sessions a bit more followable.

### The Sessions

The first session set the context — AI and blockchain converging at the infrastructure layer. The framing wasn't about hype; it was about what's actually being built in production environments.

The second session went deeper into specific technical and product territory.

![image](/images/posts/ai-infracon-blockchain-security/second-session.jpg)

Second session underway.

Honestly, the content was dense. We followed maybe 60–70% of it, and there were moments where the domain-specific terminology just went over our heads. But a few things stuck.

The thread running through most of the talks was **security**. Building at the intersection of AI and blockchain isn't just a technical challenge — it's a trust challenge. Both systems require that participants can rely on the infrastructure behaving as expected, especially when real assets and automated decisions are involved.

The company that stood out most was **Midnight** — a blockchain platform focused on building a secure, privacy-preserving foundation for AI and Web3 applications. The core idea was that you can't build serious AI-integrated financial infrastructure without a reliable, tamper-resistant base layer. Midnight was positioning itself as that base.

### What I Took Away

I won't pretend I came out of AI Infra-con as a blockchain expert. I didn't. But I got a clearer picture of where the industry is heading — specifically, the idea that AI agents will need secure, verifiable infrastructure to operate in real-world financial contexts.

The pre-study was worth it. Without it, I probably would have understood even less. And attending with someone else made the experience better — having a person to compare notes with (and share a drink afterward) is underrated.

If another conference like this comes up, I'd go again.
